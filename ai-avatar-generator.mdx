[AI Avatar](https://klyra.ai/apps/ai-avatar-generator) brings your digital avatars to life and helps you create professional videos without a traditional recording workflow. Choose an avatar, add a voice and script, configure the video, and generate the final result from one workspace.

Klyra AI's AI Avatar experience supports both **AI Avatar Photos** and **AI Avatar Videos**, powered by HeyGen. The app includes prebuilt avatar assets, custom avatar options, a large voice library, customizable video settings, and background controls. 

<Note>
  AI Avatar is designed to keep the video creation workflow simple: choose an avatar, provide the content, configure the presentation, and generate the video.
</Note>

## What you can create

AI Avatar has two main creation workflows:

| Workflow | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Video from AI Avatar Photos** | Create an avatar video from a still AI avatar image or your own avatar photo. |
| **Create Video from AI Avatar Videos** | Create a video using a prebuilt or uploaded avatar video asset. |

The AI Avatar app also provides access to avatar and voice assets, uploads, and generated video results. 

## How AI Avatar works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an avatar source">
    Select an AI Avatar Photo or an AI Avatar Video from the available assets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the voice and script">
    Choose a voice and enter the words your avatar should speak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the video">
    Set the title, dimensions, avatar presentation, and background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the video">
    Click **Generate Video** and let Klyra create the final avatar video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Select your voice and enter script text

After selecting the avatar, move to **Step 2: Select your voice and enter script text**.

#### Choose an Audio Voice

Use the **Audio Voice** selector to choose the voice your avatar will use.

The interface also provides a voice preview control so you can listen to the selected voice.

The AI Avatar product supports a large voice library covering more than 700 voices across 170\+ languages. 

#### Enter your Video Script Text

Enter the exact message you want the avatar to speak in **Video Script Text**.

A good script should:

- Start with the main message.
- Use short, natural sentences.
- Match the selected avatar and voice.
- Avoid unnecessary filler.
- End with a clear next step when appropriate.

Example:

> Hi, I'm your AI avatar. Welcome to Klyra AI, the AI Operating System that brings AI-powered work together in one intelligent workspace.

<Note>
  Keep the script focused on one message. Shorter, well-structured scripts are generally easier to review and refine before generation.
</Note>

## Photo avatars vs. video avatars

The two workflows are closely related, but they begin with different avatar assets.

|  | AI Avatar Photos | AI Avatar Videos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting asset | Still avatar image | Avatar video |
| Avatar selection | Image Avatar gallery | Video Avatar gallery |
| Voice | Selectable | Selectable |
| Script | Entered by user | Entered by user |
| Video title | Available | Available |
| Video dimension | Available | Available |
| Background color | Available | Available |
| Background image | Available | Available |
| Background image URL | Available | Available |
| Avatar-specific controls | Crop style, talking style, expression | Avatar style |

<Note>
  The settings shown above are based on the provided Klyra AI interfaces. Individual controls may vary as the AI Avatar experience evolves.
</Note>

## Best practices

### Match the avatar to the message

Choose an avatar whose appearance and presentation style fit your intended audience and use case.

### Keep scripts focused

Avoid putting multiple unrelated messages into one video. A focused script makes the final video easier to understand.

### Choose the voice deliberately

The AI Avatar experience provides a large voice library. Use the voice selector and preview controls to find a voice that fits your content. 

### Use backgrounds intentionally

A clean background can work well for explainers and business presentations, while a branded image can help support marketing content.

### Review before publishing

Review the generated video before sharing it publicly. Check the script, pronunciation, visual presentation, background, and overall message.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot find the avatar I want

Check the relevant avatar gallery:

- **Image Avatars** for still-image assets.
- **Video Avatars** for video assets.

If you want a personal avatar, use the supported upload workflow for your own photos. 

### My video does not have the presentation style I expected

Review the settings before generating:

- Avatar Crop Style
- Avatar Talking Style
- Avatar Expression
- Avatar Style
- Video Dimension
- Background

The available controls depend on whether you are creating from an AI Avatar Photo or AI Avatar Video.

### I want to use my own avatar

Klyra's AI Avatar feature supports generating a personal avatar by uploading your own profile photos. 

### I want to change the voice

Return to the **Audio Voice** selector and choose another available voice. The product supports a large multilingual voice library.
