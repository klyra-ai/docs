Create professional, high-impact presentations from a simple idea, topic, or brief. [AI Presentation](https://klyra.ai/apps/ai-presentation) can generate complete slide decks, organize content into polished layouts, add relevant visuals, and prepare the presentation for PPTX download. The app is designed to handle content, structure, and visual presentation in one workflow.

## What you can create

AI Presentation is useful for:

- Business presentations
- Pitch decks
- Marketing reports
- Business updates
- Educational materials
- Other structured presentations that need clear content and professional visual design

AI Presentation is designed to generate a complete presentation from a simple text description, while automatically handling titles, key messages, summaries, layouts, typography, and visuals.

## Before you begin

Prepare a short description of the presentation you want to create.

A strong prompt should include:

- **Topic:** What the presentation is about
- **Audience:** Who will read or watch it
- **Purpose:** What the presentation should communicate
- **Key points:** Important sections, examples, benefits, challenges, or recommendations
- **Desired outcome:** What the audience should understand or do after the presentation

### Example prompt

> Create a presentation explaining how artificial intelligence is transforming modern businesses, including key use cases, benefits, challenges, real-world examples, and what businesses should do to prepare for the future.

You do not need to write the individual slides yourself. AI Presentation uses your brief to structure the deck.

## Create a presentation

### 1. Open AI Presentation

Open **AI Presentation** from your Klyra AI workspace.

You will see the presentation creation area at the top of the page and your existing presentations below it.

### 2. Enter your presentation brief

Click inside **Create a presentation for** and describe what you want the presentation to cover.

Be specific about the subject and the information you want included. A clear brief gives the AI enough context to organize the presentation around your intended outcome.

For example:

> Create a presentation explaining how artificial intelligence is transforming modern businesses, including key use cases, benefits, challenges, real-world examples, and what businesses should do to prepare for the future.

### 3. Choose the language

Use the **Language** option to select the language for the presentation.

In the example workflow, the language is set to **English**.

### 4. Choose a presentation style

Use **Klyra Light** to control the presentation theme.

The available styling options may vary depending on the current product configuration.

Choose the style that best fits the presentation you are creating.

### 5. Set the number of slides

Use the slide-count control to select how many slides you want the presentation to contain.

The example workflow uses **7 slides**.

A shorter deck is useful when the presentation needs to stay focused. A larger deck can be useful when the topic requires more sections or supporting detail.

### 6. Set the generation level

Use the **Medium** control to select the available generation level for the presentation.

Choose the setting that best matches how much variation or creative interpretation you want from the generated deck.

### 7. Enable AI Images when needed

Use **AI Images** to control whether the presentation includes AI-generated visuals.

When enabled, AI Presentation can create relevant visuals for the generated slides.

When disabled, the presentation can be generated without AI-generated images.

In the example workflow, AI Images is enabled.

### Format

Use **Format** to control the presentation format available in the app.

### Card Split

Use **Card Split** to control how presentation content is divided into cards or sections.

### Text Tone

Use **Text Tone** to guide the writing style of the presentation.

For example, a business presentation may benefit from a professional and direct tone.

### Text Audience

Use **Text Audience** to tell AI Presentation who the presentation is intended for.

Defining the audience helps the generated content stay appropriate for the people who will view the deck.

### Instructions

Use **Instructions** when you have additional requirements that do not fit naturally into the main presentation brief.

Keep instructions focused on the result you want. Avoid adding unnecessary technical instructions.

## Generate the presentation

Once your brief and settings are ready, select the **arrow button** on the right side of the creation box.

AI Presentation will generate the presentation based on your brief and selected settings.

The result is added to **My Presentations**.

## Find your presentation

Your generated presentations appear in **My Presentations**.

Each presentation card shows information such as:

- Number of slides
- Presentation title
- Time since creation
- Generation level

You can also use **Search** to find a presentation or select **View All** to browse your presentations.

## Review your presentation

Opening a presentation takes you to the presentation viewer.

The viewer provides:

- A slide thumbnail panel on the left
- The currently selected slide in the main canvas
- Slide navigation controls
- A slide counter
- Presentation title
- **Rename** action
- **Download** action
- Fullscreen control

<Frame>
  ![AI Presentation viewer](/images/ai-presentation/03-presentation-viewer.webp)

### Navigate between slides

Select a slide thumbnail from the left panel to jump directly to that slide.

You can also use the navigation arrows to move through the presentation.

The slide counter at the top shows your current position, such as **1 / 7**.

### Review the generated structure

Before downloading, review the complete deck for:

- Accuracy of the content
- Logical flow between slides
- Titles and key messages
- Visual relevance
- Readability
- Consistency
- Whether the deck matches your original objective

AI Presentation is intended to handle content creation, layout design, typography, and visuals automatically, but reviewing the final result helps ensure it matches your specific business or communication goal.

## Rename a presentation

If you need to change the presentation title, select **Rename** in the presentation viewer.

Choose a clear title that makes the presentation easy to identify later.

## Download your presentation

When the presentation is ready, select **Download**.

AI Presentation supports export and sharing in **PPTX format**, allowing you to continue working with the presentation in compatible presentation software.

You can also use the **Download** action directly from a presentation card in **My Presentations**.

## Tips for better presentations

### Start with the outcome

Instead of writing only:

> Artificial intelligence

write:

> Create a presentation explaining how artificial intelligence is transforming modern businesses and what business leaders should do next.

The second prompt gives AI Presentation a clear outcome and scope.

### Include the audience

Tell the AI who the presentation is for.

For example:

> Create a presentation for small business owners explaining practical ways to use AI to improve productivity and customer service.

### Define the important sections

If specific topics must appear, include them in the prompt.

For example:

> Cover use cases, benefits, challenges, real-world examples, and recommendations for getting started.

### Let the AI handle the structure

You do not need to describe every slide unless a specific slide structure is important to you.

Start with the goal, audience, and key information. Let AI Presentation organize the content into a coherent deck.

### Review before downloading

Always review the generated presentation before using it externally. Check the content, visuals, ordering, and overall message against your original objective.

## Example workflow

A typical workflow looks like this:

1. Open **AI Presentation**.
2. Enter a clear presentation brief.
3. Select the language.
4. Choose the presentation style.
5. Set the number of slides.
6. Select the generation level.
7. Turn **AI Images** on or off.
8. Optionally open **Advanced Options**.
9. Add any additional instructions.
10. Select the **arrow** to generate.
11. Open the generated presentation from **My Presentations**.
12. Review the slides.
13. Rename the presentation if needed.
14. Download the finished presentation in PPTX format.

## Frequently asked questions

### What can AI Presentation create?

AI Presentation can create complete slide decks from simple text, topics, or briefs. It is suitable for pitch decks, marketing reports, business updates, educational materials, and other professional presentations.

### Does AI Presentation create the slide content?

Yes. The app can intelligently create titles, bullet points, key messages, and summaries based on your presentation brief.

### Can AI Presentation create visuals?

Yes. The app includes built-in AI image generation for relevant presentation visuals. You can control this with the **AI Images** option.

### Can I choose how many slides to generate?

Yes. The creation interface includes a slide-count control.

### Can I customize the presentation?

Yes. The creation interface provides language, theme, slide count, generation level, AI Images, and additional Advanced Options. The exact options available can change as the product evolves.

### Can I download the presentation?

Yes. AI Presentation supports downloading the generated presentation in PPTX format.

### Where can I find presentations I created earlier?

Previously generated presentations appear under **My Presentations**. You can search your presentations or select **View All**.
