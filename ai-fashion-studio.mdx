AI Fashion Studio turns fashion concepts into professional visuals without requiring a traditional photo shoot. You can build a complete photoshoot from wardrobe items, dress a model with Virtual Try-On, swap models while preserving the scene, and turn still images into cinematic video keyframes.

This tutorial covers the workflows shown in the AI Fashion Studio interface.

## What you can do

AI Fashion Studio brings several fashion workflows into one workspace:

- **Photoshoot Studio**: combine wardrobe items with a model, pose, background, lighting, and aspect ratio.
- **Virtual Try-On Studio**: upload a model and add garments slot-by-slot.
- **Change Model**: replace the model while keeping the outfit, pose, and scene.
- **Create Video**: turn a fashion still into a cinematic keyframe using a motion preset.
- **My Wardrobe**: keep reusable fashion products organized in one library.
- **My Photoshoots**: browse and manage generated fashion creations.

The interface also includes **Change Style** and **Edit Image** for additional creative variations.

## Open AI Fashion Studio

From the Klyra AI workspace, open **AI Fashion Studio**.

The main screen gives you a quick overview of your fashion workspace, including photoshoots, video keyframes, wardrobe items, favorites, and the available AI tools.

![AI Fashion Studio dashboard](/images/ai-fashion-studio/fashion-studio-dashboard.webp)

From the dashboard, you can start a new photoshoot, open Virtual Try-On Studio, or choose another fashion workflow.

## Create a photoshoot

Photoshoot Studio lets you combine products from your wardrobe and generate a complete fashion scene.

### 1. Select your products

In **Pick your products**, choose up to three wardrobe items.

You can select items from **My Wardrobe**, upload new items, or generate an item with AI.

The example below has two products selected.

![Photoshoot Studio product selection](/images/ai-fashion-studio/photoshoot-studio.webp)

### 2. Configure the shoot

The setup panel provides controls for:

- **Products**
- **Model**
- **Pose**
- **Background**
- **Lighting**
- **Aspect Ratio**
- **Additional direction**

Model, pose, and background can remain on **Auto** when you want Klyra AI to handle those choices.

For lighting, the interface shown uses **Soft**.

### 3. Choose an aspect ratio

Select the format that matches where you plan to use the image.

The available options shown are:

- 1:1
- 4:3
- 3:4
- 16:9
- 9:16

### 4. Add additional direction

Use **Additional direction** when you want to give the generation extra creative guidance.

For example, you can describe a specific visual action or atmosphere. Keep the instruction focused on what you want the final fashion image to communicate.

### 5. Generate the photoshoot

Click **Generate Photoshoot**.

Klyra AI creates the fashion visual using the selected wardrobe and creative settings.

The generated result appears in the **Generated Results** area.

![Generated fashion photoshoot result](/images/ai-fashion-studio/fashion-studio-result.webp)

## Use Virtual Try-On Studio

Virtual Try-On Studio is designed for dressing a model with garments from separate product images.

It uses a slot-based workflow so you can build an outfit piece by piece.

![Virtual Try-On Studio](/images/ai-fashion-studio/virtual-try-on.webp)

### 1. Upload the model

Under **The Model**, upload the model photograph.

You can drag an image into the upload area or use **browse**.

The interface accepts:

- PNG
- JPG
- WEBP
- Files up to 20 MB

### 2. Add garments to slots

The **Garment Slots** section provides five available categories:

- **Top**
- **Bottom**
- **Outerwear**
- **Footwear**
- **Accessory**

Select a slot to add the corresponding garment.

This lets you combine separate product images into one complete outfit.

### 3. Set the creative direction

The **Creative Direction** section lets you define the visual treatment.

#### Choose a pose

The interface includes pose presets such as:

- Runway Strut
- Editorial Portrait
- Seated Luxe
- Power Pose
- Candid Moment
- Hero Shot
- Back Shot
- In Motion
- Wall Lean
- Beauty Close-Up

Pose selection is optional.

#### Choose a background

You can select from studio and environmental backgrounds including:

- White Studio
- Grey Studio
- Black Studio
- Blush Pastel
- Open Sky
- Golden Beach
- Urban Street
- Luxury Interior
- Wild Nature

Background selection is optional.

#### Choose lighting

Available lighting presets shown in the interface include:

- Soft Studio
- Golden Hour
- Dramatic Side
- Rim Light
- Overcast
- Neon Gel

### 4. Generate the look

Once the model, garments, and creative direction are ready, click **Generate Look**.

Klyra AI creates the dressed model while applying the selected creative settings.

## Change the model

**Change Model** lets you replace the model in an existing fashion image while preserving the rest of the visual direction.

![Change Model](/images/ai-fashion-studio/change-model.webp)

### 1. Upload the original photograph

Under **Original Photograph**, upload the fashion image you want to modify.

You can drag the image into the upload area or use **browse**.

Supported formats shown in the interface:

- PNG
- JPG
- WEBP
- Up to 20 MB

### 2. Choose the new model

Under **New Model Portrait**, upload a portrait of the model you want to use.

This field is optional in the interface because you can instead select a preset model.

### 3. Use a preset model

The available preset choices shown are:

- Random Model
- Tall & Elegant
- Athletic
- Curvy
- Streetwear
- Mature

Select a preset when you do not want to upload a separate portrait.

### 4. Preserve the original

Two preservation controls are available:

- **Keep original outfit**
- **Keep original background**

Both are enabled in the example interface.

These controls let you specify which parts of the original image should remain unchanged while the model is replaced.

### 5. Generate

Click **Generate**.

The new result appears in **Generated Results**.

The workflow is designed specifically to change the model while keeping the outfit, pose, and scene consistent.

## Create a fashion video keyframe

The **Create Video** workflow transforms a still fashion image into a cinematic motion concept.

![Create Video](/images/ai-fashion-studio/create-video.webp)

### 1. Upload the start image

Under **Start Image**, upload the first frame.

The interface accepts:

- PNG
- JPG
- WEBP
- Up to 20 MB

### 2. Choose a motion preset

The available motion presets shown are:

- Runway Walk
- Fabric Twirl
- Windblown
- Slow Zoom In
- Dolly Pan
- Jacket Reveal

Choose the preset that best matches the movement you want to create.

### 3. Adjust motion intensity

Use the **Motion intensity** slider to control how strongly the selected motion should be expressed.

The example screen shows the intensity set to **3**.

### 4. Describe the video

The optional **Describe your video** field lets you add additional direction.

For example, you can describe the camera movement, model action, or overall cinematic behavior you want the keyframe to communicate.

### 5. Generate the keyframe

Click **Generate Video Keyframe**.

The generated result appears in **Generated Keyframe**.

## Manage your fashion assets

AI Fashion Studio is designed around reusable fashion assets.

The dashboard provides access to:

- **My Wardrobe** for organizing clothing and fashion products.
- **My Photoshoots** for browsing generated fashion creations.
- **Edit Image** for refining generated images.
- **Change Style** for creating alternate outfit aesthetics.
- **Create Video** for turning still fashion imagery into motion concepts.

Keeping products and generated work organized makes it easier to reuse the same assets across multiple creative workflows.

## Tips for better results

### Use clean source images

Upload clear product, garment, model, and portrait images. A clean source makes it easier for the AI to preserve the important visual details.

### Be intentional with pose and background

For fashion campaigns, select a pose and background that support the purpose of the image. Editorial portraits, runway movement, and lifestyle scenes can communicate very different visual directions.

### Reuse wardrobe items

Keep frequently used garments in **My Wardrobe** so they are available for future photoshoots and Virtual Try-On workflows.

### Add focused direction

When using an additional direction or video description, describe the specific result you want instead of adding unnecessary instructions.

## Quick workflow

For a fast fashion campaign:

1. Open **AI Fashion Studio**.
2. Add products to **My Wardrobe** if they are not already available.
3. Open **Photoshoot** and select up to three products.
4. Choose the model, pose, background, lighting, and aspect ratio.
5. Generate the photoshoot.
6. Use **Change Model** when you need another model with the same visual setup.
7. Use **Virtual Try-On** when you need to combine separate garments into an outfit.
8. Use **Create Video** when you want to turn a still into a cinematic motion keyframe.
9. Keep the resulting work in **My Photoshoots** for reuse and management.
