## How Klyra AI works

Klyra AI is designed around a simple idea:

> **Start with the outcome you want. Klyra AI brings the AI capabilities together to help you get there.**

Each AI App is designed for a specific type of work, while everything remains part of the same AI Operating System.

A typical workflow looks like this:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your goal">
    Decide what you want to accomplish, such as creating content, generating an image, producing a video, or building a chatbot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right AI App">
    Select the Klyra AI application designed for the type of work you want to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide your input">
    Give the application your instructions, text, images, files, or other required information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your result">
    Let Klyra AI process your request and generate the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine and reuse">
    Improve your result, create variations, download your work, or use it as part of another workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You don't need to understand which underlying AI model is best for every task. Klyra AI brings leading AI models together so you can focus on the work you want to accomplish.
</Note>

## Understanding AI Capacity

Klyra AI uses **AI Capacity** to represent the amount of AI-powered work available to you.

Different types of AI work can consume different amounts of capacity. Text generation, image generation, video creation, and voice workflows can each require different amounts of AI processing.

You don't need to manage the underlying technical systems.

Klyra AI keeps this complexity behind the scenes so you can focus on your work.

As your needs grow, you can increase your AI Capacity rather than unlocking individual AI Apps.

<Info>
  Paid plans include the complete AI Operating System. Plans primarily differ by AI Capacity, team members, and business scale.
</Info>
